01 / DESIGN PROCESS

A clearer way
to plan.

Every space begins with a plan. Visualization gives that plan perspective.

Understanding an interior goes beyond its dimensions. Proportion, circulation, furniture placement and natural light all influence how a space functions and feels.

ROUWA explores these relationships through digital design and 3D visualization, transforming architectural ideas into detailed visual concepts that help communicate atmosphere, proportion and spatial possibilities.

03 / THE STUDIO

DESIGN THROUGH VISUALIZATION

Beyond
the render.

ROUWA is an independent interior visualization and spatial design initiative, focused on the relationship between architectural ideas and their visual development.

The studio's direction brings together the disciplines of interior design, architectural planning and digital visualization. Its approach is built around clear spatial thinking and the exchange of perspectives between design disciplines.

Current explorations focus on residential environments, interior layouts and architectural detailing, with particular attention to how digital visualization can support design development and communicate spatial ideas before construction.
